About me

I am a software engineer (Tecnológico de Antioquia), a university lecturer with a diploma in pedagogy, and a student in the Specialization in Artificial Intelligence at UNIR. I am a public servant of the Judicial Branch of Colombia.

I build free software for court document management and I teach how to automate court procedures responsibly. I am the author of AgilEx (MIT license, 2022), and I was a member of team Marduk, winner of the 1st JusticIALab Innovation Contest 2024 in the Data-Driven Justice category (source, in Spanish). I published in Cuaderno Activa (2024).

Sprint Judicial is my personal free-software and outreach project. It is not a business: I do not sell, charge, or accept sponsorships or donations.

My motto is “Help people think more like humans and less like machines”. Outside work, I find my center in the water.

Personal opinions; I do not represent the Judicial Branch. I do not comment on cases or give advice. Teaching, research and free software.
